How to use this activity

Guided review offers hints and worked explanations. Engineering practice asks for more independent reasoning. The final readiness check mixes 20 original problems without hints until each answer is submitted. Numerical answers accept small rounding differences. This is practice, not a graded Canvas quiz. A score of 80% is a suggested readiness target, not a guarantee of assessment performance.
